OUR STORY

Built to close the gap between sold and delivered

In 2025, a lot of businesses were spending money on services and tools from freelancers and agencies that looked impressive in a demo but did very little once real work began. The tools themselves were not the problem. Most providers sold a product, installed it, and moved on, with no proper integration, no training for the team, no or least goals focus and no one to fix things when the setup stopped working.

Badar Edge was started to close that gap. We begin with the one problem that is costing your business revenue, a service that achieve goals, build a system around it, and then monitor and improve it every month. We put results and goals first and technology second.
