Delaney Realty

James Delaney · Florida, USA

"We were losing leads because we couldn't respond fast enough. Speed-to-Lead cut response time from hours to seconds and our conversion jumped 38%."

Before

After

45-second response, conversion up 38%, four additional deals closed every month.
