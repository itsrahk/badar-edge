Dental Clinic

From Website Traffic to Patient Appointments

Website · Appointment System · WhatsApp Integration · Local SEO

Aurelia Dental Care
Dental Clinic
Website, Appointment System, WhatsApp Integration, Local SEO
6 Weeks
Website that did nothing — visitors landed and left with no reason to stay
No online booking — every appointment required a phone call
30% of calls going to voicemail, turning potential patients away
Zero SEO presence — invisible on Google for local dental searches
Manual scheduling consuming front desk hours every day

We started with the booking flow, not the design. Every decision we made was filtered through one question: does this help a patient book faster? The clinic did not need a prettier website. It needed a system that turned visitors into booked appointments without anyone picking up a phone.

  • 01

    Conversion-Focused Website

    A clean, fast website designed around one goal — getting visitors to book. Every page funnels toward the appointment CTA with zero distractions.

  • 02

    Online Appointment System

    A real-time booking system that lets patients pick their preferred time, fills open slots, and sends instant confirmations via email and WhatsApp.

  • 03

    WhatsApp Integration

    Automated appointment reminders, follow-up messages, and two-way communication so patients never miss a visit and the front desk stays unburdened.

  • 04

    Local SEO Foundation

    Google Business Profile optimization, local schema markup, and geo-targeted content so the clinic appears when patients search for nearby dental care.

42 → 91 Monthly Appointments
+117% Appointment Growth
3.2x More Enquiries
Before

Static website with no booking, 30% voicemail rate, zero local search visibility, and a front desk overwhelmed with manual scheduling.

After

91 monthly appointments booked online, WhatsApp automations handling confirmations and reminders, and the clinic ranking on the first page of local search.

Real Estate

Turning Property Traffic Into Qualified Leads

Property Platform · CRM · Lead Automation · WhatsApp Integration

Meridian Properties
Real Estate
Property Platform, CRM, Lead Automation, WhatsApp Integration
8 Weeks
Property listings scattered across multiple platforms with no centralized system
Leads sitting for 6+ hours before anyone responded
Deals lost daily to faster competitors who replied first
No qualification process — agents wasted time on unqualified inquiries
Zero automation — every follow-up required manual effort

The problem was lead speed, not lead volume. Every inquiry needed to be captured instantly, qualified automatically, and routed to the right agent before the buyer moved on. We designed a system where the first response happens in seconds, not hours.

  • 01

    Property Discovery Experience

    A fast, filterable property platform where buyers search by location, budget, and property type. Every listing leads to a clear inquiry action.

  • 02

    Lead Capture System

    Smart capture forms on every property page that collect buyer intent data, budget range, and timeline — feeding directly into the CRM.

  • 03

    CRM + Lead Qualification

    Automated lead scoring that ranks inquiries by intent and budget. Hot leads route instantly to senior agents. Warm leads enter a nurture sequence.

  • 04

    Automated Follow-Up

    WhatsApp and email sequences that follow up at the right intervals, answer common questions, and keep leads warm until they are ready to act.

6 hrs → 45 sec Response Time
+38% Lead-to-Appointment Rate
+4 deals/mo Additional Closed Deals
Before

6-hour response times, leads going cold, and commission revenue leaking to faster competitors every single day.

After

45-second average response, 38% more leads converting to appointments, and four additional deals closed every month.

“The custom workflow routes every inquiry to the right rep instantly. We went from losing deals because we were too slow to closing four extra deals a month. The system pays for itself.”

Ecommerce
Store Redesign, CRO, Checkout Optimization, Analytics

68% cart abandonment rate — shoppers adding to cart and never completing checkout
Low conversion rate despite consistent traffic — visitors not turning into buyers
Complicated checkout flow with too many steps and friction points
Weak product pages failing to communicate value or build trust
No analytics visibility — unable to identify where the funnel was breaking

We mapped the entire purchase journey from ad click to order confirmation. Every friction point became a priority. We did not redesign for aesthetics — we redesigned to remove every reason a buyer would bail. The store needed to convert, not just look good.

  • 04
