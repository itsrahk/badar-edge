Privacy Policy

How Badar Edge collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Badar Edge ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at itsrahk.github.io/badar-edge and provides AI solutions and services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact information — name, email, and phone/WhatsApp number you provide when submitting a form or contacting us.
  • Business details — information you share about your business, challenges, and requirements.
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics data (pages visited, device type) collected via Google Analytics to improve our site.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To schedule and conduct consultations and growth audits.
  • To prepare proposals and deliver contracted services.
  • To improve our website, services, and user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics and a Search Console verification tag to understand how visitors use our site. These tools may place cookies or use similar technologies. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only with service providers that help us operate our site and deliver services (for example, form processing and analytics providers), and only as necessary to provide those services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at itsraiarslan@gmail.com.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as WhatsApp and LinkedIn). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their policies before providing any information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "Last updated" date shown above.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at itsraiarslan@gmail.com.
