DealFlow The Lead-to-Deal System for Real Estate Real estate agencies, developers, brokerages and agents

The commercial case. Property interest arrives through portals, social ads, Google, WhatsApp, phone calls, walk-ins, referrals and project campaigns. The useful next step is usually a real conversation: confirm what the person wants, share accurate listing details, answer practical questions, and arrange a call or visit. DealFlow gives the team one consistent route for that work and shows which sources lead to serious opportunities.

The problem

An inquiry may reach a salesperson's phone, a page inbox, a portal account and a call log, with no shared record. A prospect asks about price, payment plan, possession timing, location or documentation and gets a slow or incomplete reply. Published inventory changes while old promotional content keeps running. The result is repeated effort, unclear ownership and low trust. A high inquiry count does not prove the campaign produces visits or sales, and buying more traffic before fixing response and information quality only adds wasted follow-up.

What we diagnose

We map the journey for the relevant city, project or property type and review a sample of inquiries from each major channel. We ask:

  • Which city, area, property type, budget band and buyer profile are the priorities?
  • Who owns a new inquiry from a portal, social inbox, web form, call or message?
  • How quickly does the team respond, and what happens when the assigned person is unavailable?
  • Are current inventory, prices, payment plans and possession dates verified before they are published?
  • What makes an inquiry qualified: location, budget, timeline, intended use, payment readiness or a requested visit?
  • Can the team report booked calls, visits attended, reservations and completed deals by source?
  • Which languages and contact methods does each audience actually prefer, and where is that recorded?

SourceAccurate listing informationTwo-way contactNeeds understoodCall or visitReservation or transaction

What we build

DealFlow creates one shared inquiry path without forcing every audience into a single channel. A person can call, message, use social, a portal or a web form. The inquiry is recorded with its source and the contact's preference, assigned to a named person and given a clear next action. Staff answer with current, approved information and record whether the person wants a call, details or a visit. Automation can acknowledge and remind. A person handles pricing questions, negotiation, verification and exceptions. AI-assisted drafting or sorting is human-reviewed and never invents property facts.

Websites, portals, SEO, social, paid media, content, CRM, automation and AI are tools inside the system when they solve a diagnosed problem. DealFlow is the working lead-to-deal process the team operates.

JobFlow The Lead-to-Job System for Home Services HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing and other service businesses

The commercial case. Demand is only valuable when the business can answer, understand the work, schedule the right crew and follow through. JobFlow connects marketing to the booked job, so owners can see which opportunities become revenue and where the handoff breaks.

The problem

Homeowners call after finding the company in local search, a directory, a referral, a yard sign or an advertisement. Some calls arrive while technicians are working. Some jobs sit outside the service area or are not a fit. Requests get handled through a shared phone, a paper note and a field-service app that do not connect. A technician finishes a job and nobody asks for a review or schedules the next maintenance visit. The business can be busy and still lose profitable work. Missed calls, slow callbacks, weak service-area filtering, incomplete job notes and unscheduled estimates waste demand and dispatch time. More leads create longer waits and worse service if capacity and routing are ignored.

What we diagnose

We inspect a sample of calls, web requests, estimates, booked jobs and completed work. We ask:

  • Which services, job types, locations and hours can the team reliably cover?
  • How many calls are answered live, and how quickly are missed calls returned?
  • How are emergency requests separated from routine work and routed to a qualified person?
  • What is needed to estimate or dispatch: address, issue, equipment, access, urgency and customer preferences?
  • How many estimates are sent, accepted, scheduled, completed and paid?
  • Which sources create jobs with healthy value and margin, not just high call volume?
  • Where do dispatch, technicians, office staff and follow-up lose information?

What we build

JobFlow gives every opportunity an owner and a practical next step. It captures calls and forms, checks service and location fit, routes the request, books an estimate or a job, sends the right reminders and records the outcome. Managers can connect lead source to completed work and see where the office-to-field handoff needs attention. Automation can acknowledge a request, collect non-sensitive job details and send a booking link. It does not make unsafe technical judgements or quote complex work without approved rules. A trained person handles emergencies, estimates, customer questions and exceptions.

Websites, local SEO, Google listings, paid search, social, content, CRM, automation, AI and integrations are components selected to solve a specific problem. JobFlow is the functioning lead-to-job process.

PatientFlow The Patient Acquisition and Conversion System for Clinics Independent clinics and multi-location practices

The commercial case. Patients may find a clinic through a doctor referral, Google, social media, a local directory, a phone call or a message. They need clear information about the doctor, specialty, location, hours and how to ask for an appointment. PatientFlow connects those first questions to a staff-owned booking process while protecting clinical judgment and patient dignity.

The problem

A prospective patient may message after hours, call while the front desk is busy, or ask a basic question through a social inbox. The clinic replies late or gives different answers in different places. A patient may not know which doctor offers the service, where the clinic is, what the appointment process involves, or whether there is availability. A busy practice can receive plenty of attention and still make access hard. The clinic may count calls, followers and messages without knowing how many appropriate requests were reached, booked or attended. Staff repeat directions and details, and patient information may end up in tools not suited to health data. The commercial goal is a clearer patient access path, not pressure to buy care.

What we diagnose

We review how a patient request moves through the clinic and how staff can respond. We ask:

  • Which specialties, providers, locations and appointment types are the priorities?
  • Are doctor names, qualifications, registration details, hours, location and services accurate and easy to confirm?
  • How do calls, messages, referrals and web inquiries reach the right staff member?
  • What is the expected response time, including evenings and holidays, and who handles urgent clinical concerns?
  • What counts as an appropriate request, a booked appointment, an attended visit and a new patient?
  • Which channels create attended appointments and which mainly create unanswered messages?
  • What patient information is collected in marketing, booking and clinical systems, and who can access it?

Search, referral or messageClinic and doctor informationAdministrative responseAppointment choiceAttended visit

What we build

PatientFlow helps patients get reliable service and appointment information, reach the right person and complete the steps to attend. It creates clear contact routes, response ownership, booking options, confirmations and staff reminders. The clinic can review which sources support access and whether its teams have the capacity to respond. We use the language patients actually prefer. A messaging channel works as an administrative route only when the clinic has approved it, clear staff ownership and suitable boundaries for sensitive information. Automation can acknowledge a request or remind someone of an appointment. It does not diagnose or give treatment advice. Clinicians review every medical claim and patient education item.

Websites, search, marketing, social, content, CRM, automation, AI and integrations are components only when a specific access or measurement gap calls for them. PatientFlow is the patient access process, built around appropriate care.
